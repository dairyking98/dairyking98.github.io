When the plates start moving¶

Element movement against key depth, for four sizes of move on a top-row key. The tip travels free until it reaches the nearer plate, so a short move is picked up late in the stroke and a long move picks up almost at once. Every line then rises at the same slope, which is why the mass at the keytop is the same for every move.

Tolerance stack¶

The error at the element in columns, from each source. The tapered lock can correct ±0.4 column, shown as the dashed line. Drag the sliders to try other values. Only the laser edge figure is a typical shop value. The other defaults are my guesses, and the chain backlash in particular needs measuring.
