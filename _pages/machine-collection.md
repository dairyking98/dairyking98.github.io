The mechanical things that don't fit under [typewriters](/collections/typewriters/) or [pocket watches](/collections/pocket-watches/) but belong to the same habit: pre-electronic precision mechanisms, bought cheap and broken, and brought back into service.

Missing years, serial numbers, and photos mean I haven't logged that detail yet — these lists reflect what I've catalogued so far, not the full extent of what I own.

Part of the wider [collections](/collections/).

## Adding Machines & Calculators

Mechanical calculation before the pocket calculator killed it off, spanning the three families that never really merged: **Comptometer**-style full-keyboard machines you drive with all ten fingers, **pinwheel and stepped-drum** machines you crank, and **printing adders** that commit each entry to a paper tape.

## Electric Fans

Brass-bladed and japanned-steel fans from the period when an electric motor in the home was still worth building a beautiful casting around.

**A note on these:** pre-war fans have no finger guard worth the name, cloth-insulated wiring that has usually gone brittle, and no thermal cutout. Every one that runs here has been rewired. They are display and occasional-use pieces, not appliances.
