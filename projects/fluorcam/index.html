    FluorCam

    FluorCam is a low-cost, open-source fluorescence and IR dark-field microscope system based on RoboCam. The system achieves fluorescence imaging comparable to commercial microscopes at a fraction of the cost, making advanced imaging accessible for research and education.

    Project Overview

    FluorCam adapts the RoboCam platform for fluorescence imaging:

    • Coaxial Optics: Specialized optical design for fluorescence imaging
    • Raspberry Pi Imaging: High-quality imaging using Raspberry Pi camera hardware
    • Custom Python Automation: Automated acquisition and control workflows
    • Low-Cost Design: ~$500 total cost vs. $20,000+ commercial systems

    Key Features

    • Fluorescence Imaging: Comparable performance to commercial fluorescence microscopes
    • IR Dark-Field Imaging: Infrared dark-field capabilities for enhanced contrast
    • Coaxial Optics: Custom optical design optimized for fluorescence applications
    • Automated Acquisition: Python-based automation for experiment workflows
    • Cost-Effective: Dramatically lower cost than commercial alternatives
    • Based on RoboCam: Inherits motion control and automation from RoboCam platform

    Technical Implementation

    FluorCam extends RoboCam with:

    • Optical Filters: Integration of excitation and emission filters for fluorescence
    • Mounting Systems: Custom gantry alignment and optical mounts
    • Automated Acquisition: Specialized workflows for fluorescence imaging experiments
    • Mechanical Design: Custom 3D-printed components for optical alignment

    Research Impact

    FluorCam demonstrates that high-quality fluorescence imaging can be achieved with:

    • Open-source hardware and software
    • Low-cost components
    • Custom design and fabrication
    • Accessible automation tools

    Repository

    The complete source code, documentation, and setup instructions are available in the RoboCam-Suite repository, which includes the FluorCam implementation.
