Capstone project (Team-o-Matic): prevent tip-over by tracking vehicle–load **center of mass** against the **support polygon**, with embedded control, simulation, and a fabricated scale-model boom. Started Fall 2025 as a different project — a modular EV battery-swapping system — before pivoting to stability control in Spring 2026.
