    Why this one

    Sixth in my own ranking, for its age and its uniqueness.

    The Sun Typewriter Company built small, cheap machines aimed at the part of the market the big standards priced out, and the No. 2 is a three-bank design — three rows of keys rather than four, with a shift doing the work of the missing row. Compared to a contemporary Underwood it is a machine of far fewer parts, and the compromises it makes to get there are the interesting part.
