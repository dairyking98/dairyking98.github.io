    Pocket Watch Collection

    American pocket watches, catalogued the way collectors index them — by the movement, not the case. The two were sold separately for most of this era: a jeweller would fit a customer’s chosen movement into a chosen case, so the movement serial is what dates a watch and identifies its grade. Case serials, where a case is original and marked, are logged separately.

    Missing years, serial numbers, and photos mean I haven’t logged that detail yet — this list reflects what I’ve catalogued so far, not the full extent of what I own.
